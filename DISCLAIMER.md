# Disclaimer

This repository is a research and literature-synthesis project. It is **not medical advice**
and must not be used to guide the diagnosis or treatment of any person.

- Nothing here is a substitute for consultation with a qualified hematologist or oncologist.
- Treatment of acute myeloid leukemia is highly individualized and depends on genetics, fitness,
  comorbidity, transplant eligibility, and locally available therapies and trials.
- Drug approvals, labels, and guideline recommendations change. Any regulatory status stated here
  was accurate to the best of the authors' verification at the time of writing and may since have changed.
- Content was assembled with substantial assistance from an AI system (see [METHODOLOGY.md](METHODOLOGY.md)).
  It has been citation-audited but **has not been peer reviewed by domain experts**. Errors are possible
  and, at this stage, likely somewhere in a corpus of this size.

If you or someone you care about has AML: please talk to your treating team, and ask specifically
about molecular testing, measurable residual disease monitoring, transplant evaluation, and clinical
trial eligibility. Those four questions change outcomes.
